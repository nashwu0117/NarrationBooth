# Sample script

Anything above the first VO_ marker is ignored, so you can keep notes for yourself here.
Each **VO_xxx** block becomes one take, saved as VO_xxx.wav (and VO_xxx.mov in camera mode).
One sentence per line. The 「／」 mark is where to breathe.

## 01 Opening

**VO_01a**

Hi everyone ／ welcome back to the channel.
Today we're looking at why the sky is blue.

**VO_01b**

It sounds like a simple question ／ but the answer goes all the way back to 1871.

## 02 The idea

**VO_02a**

Sunlight looks white ／ but it's really every colour mixed together.
When it hits the air ／ the blue part gets scattered the most.

**VO_02b**

這一段示範中文稿 ／ 一句一行。
「／」就是換氣的地方 ／ 唸到這裡再停。
